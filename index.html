Who I am

More than
a move.

Rooted in both Chinese and Javanese traditions, this perspective comes from fully inhabiting both identities — not existing between them. That background shaped an early awareness of how movement, expression, and meaning shift across contexts, and how different systems can coexist without canceling each other out.

Over fifteen years of practicing, observing, and teaching, engagement with dance gradually shifted from participation to analysis — not only moving, but questioning how movement works, how it is learned, and why it is so often misunderstood. What began as an instinctive need to move became something more structured, more deliberate, and ultimately more meaningful.

This work is not a claim of authority. It is the result of long-term commitment — and a genuine drive to create orientation for anyone who feels drawn to movement but doesn't yet know where to begin.
